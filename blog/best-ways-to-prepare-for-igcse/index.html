Best Ways to Prepare for IGCSE Exams: A Proven Roadmap to A*

Published on May 14, 2026 • 6 min read

Preparing for IGCSE exams can feel like a daunting task, especially with the breadth of subjects and the depth of the curriculum. However, success in IGCSE (whether it's Cambridge CIE or Edexcel) isn't just about working hard—it's about working smart.

1. Master the Syllabus (Not Just the Textbook)

One of the most common mistakes students make is relying solely on their textbooks. Textbooks are great for learning, but the syllabus document is your actual roadmap. It tells you exactly what the examiners are looking for.

2. The Power of Topical Past Papers

Don't wait until the end of the year to start solving past papers. At Gradeup, we recommend solving topical PYQs immediately after finishing a chapter. This cements your understanding of how that specific topic is tested in real exams.

3. Understand the Marking Schemes

In subjects like Biology and Chemistry, keywords are everything. You might understand the concept, but if you don't use the specific terms required by the marking scheme, you may lose marks. Spend time analyzing the marking schemes to learn the "language" of the examiners.

"Excellence is not a singular act, but a habit of consistent, structured practice."

4. Create a Balanced Study Timetable

For our students in the Gulf and India, managing school hours with private study can be tough. A balanced timetable should include:

  • Active recall sessions
  • Short breaks (Pomodoro technique)
  • Dedicated slots for difficult subjects like Math or Physics
